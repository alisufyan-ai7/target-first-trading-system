# Research Objectives

## Primary objective

Determine whether a robust combination of strategies and markets can produce a daily P&L distribution with materially fewer low-output days than a single XAUUSD strategy.

## Acceptance dimensions

The project must evaluate all of the following, not only total return:

- target-first hit rate;
- expectancy after realistic costs;
- median daily P&L;
- mean daily P&L;
- percentage of days <= USD 50;
- percentage of days >= USD 100;
- percentage of days >= USD 150;
- percentage of losing days;
- maximum daily loss;
- maximum drawdown;
- consecutive low-output days;
- consecutive losing days;
- trades per day;
- opportunity concentration by market/session;
- correlation between simultaneous signals.

## Working distribution targets

These are research preferences rather than pass/fail guarantees:

- <= USD 50 days: ideally <= ~20%;
- >= USD 100 days: ideally >= ~75–80%;
- USD 150–200: desired daily stop-profit zone when opportunity exists;
- trades/day: usually 2–4, with zero forced trades.

## Non-goals

The system must not be optimized to:

- show an artificially high win rate;
- force exactly four trades every day;
- make every trade target exactly the same price distance;
- hide losing/zero days;
- use martingale or loss-recovery sizing;
- assume a risk/reward ratio implies a win probability.
